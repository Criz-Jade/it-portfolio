06 · TROUBLESHOOTING

Working Through Dependencies

One of the main lessons from the lab was that server installations depend on careful preparation. I had to pay attention to prerequisites, system resources, permissions, commands, and configuration details before moving to the next stage.

07 · WHAT I LEARNED

My Perspective

Repeating the Exchange setup helped me understand how much planning and verification are involved in real-world infrastructure work. The project required patience when blockers appeared and strengthened my problem-solving approach when troubleshooting a multi-system environment.
